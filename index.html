What is Astrodex

Astrodex is a website that provides all of the information you need for stargazing, aurora hunting, and astrophotography. The data updates live every minute, and is based on your location. It has moon data, space weather data, cloud data, and webcams.

Moon Phase

This tab shows information about the moon. It shows the moon phase, illumination, and the moonrise and moonset time. It also shows when the next full and new moons are, so you can plan ahead.

Aurora Data

This tab shows useful space weather data for tracking the aurora. It shows the KP index, the Solar Wind Speed, Proton Density, Bz and Bt. It also graphs the data, so you can see the past data at a glimpse. You can choose between 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, 6 hours, 12 hours and 24 hours.

Bz: The north - south vertical direction of the solar wind’s magnetic field. Opposite poles attract, so when the Bz points south (Bz < 0), strong storms can occur. Bz is the most important metric for auroras, as having a positive Bz can wipe out any other good data.

Bt: The total strength of the magnetic field carried by the solar wind. The higher this is, the higher the potential energy for a geomagnetic storm.

Proton Density: The amount of solar particles per cubic centimeter in the solar wind. Higher density means more particles in the atmosphere, which collide with gas molecules and create brighter, more intense auroral displays.

Solar Wind Speed: A measure of how fast solar particles are approaching the earth. Higher speeds deliver more energy to the Earth’s magnetic field, powering geomagnetic storms

KP Index: This is a global scale that ranges from 0 (lowest) to 9 (highest). This scale quantifies disturbances in the Earth’s magnetic field over a 3 hour window. KP Index indicates how far south the aurora viewing line moves.

ACE EPAM Proton Flux: This is measurements from the Electron, Proton and Alpha Monitor on NASA’s ACE satellite. This tracks the protons moving approaching Earth. When the protons start rising, it is usually a good indicator that a CME is incoming.

The Aurora Score

The Aurora Score is a number calculated based on all of the space weather data. This number provides an at a glimpse guage on where the aurora can be seen from. It was created using historical data, and where historical auroras could be seen from. It goes from 0 - 100, with 0 being the worst data imaginable, and 100 being the best. Cloud Cover

This tab shows useful information about cloud cover and visibility. Simply enter your location, and the data will update. This tab has cloud cover, temperature, wind speed, humidity and visibility. It also has a 24 hour future forecast, so you can plan ahead.

Webcams

This tab has live webcams from different latitudes across North America, Europe and Asia. These can be used to gauge if conditions are favorable at your latitude.
